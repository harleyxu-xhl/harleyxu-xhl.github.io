A structured 6-week journey to natural sleep.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I) is the gold standard, first-line treatment recommended by clinical sleep medicine.

  • Week 1

    Week 1: Sleep Baseline & The Diary

    Log bedtimes, wake times, and night awakenings. Discover your true baseline sleep efficiency without judgment or pressure.

  • Week 2

    Week 2: Sleep Restriction Therapy

    Temporarily match your time in bed to your actual sleep duration. Rebuild intense, natural homeostatic sleep pressure.

  • Week 3

    Week 3: Stimulus Control Protocol

    Break the subconscious link between your bed and frustration or wakefulness. The strict 20-minute rule: if awake, get out of bed.

  • Week 4

    Week 4: Cognitive Restructuring

    Dismantle catastrophic sleep anxiety ('If I don't sleep 8 hours, tomorrow is ruined') using clinically proven cognitive reframing.

  • Week 5

    Week 5: Circadian Rhythm & Sleep Hygiene

    Anchor your morning wake-up time, synchronize sunlight exposure, calibrate bedroom temperature, and set caffeine cutoffs.

  • Week 6

    Week 6: Relapse Prevention & Graduation

    Lock in lifelong healthy sleep habits. Review your transformation report, sign your graduation pledge, and keep your certificate.
