CooldownPhone temperature for Android

Know why your phone gets hot.

Cooldown shows your battery temperature, alerts you when your phone overheats, and tells you what is heating it up. Low-temperature mode can also dim the screen for you.

Free. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How hot is too hot?

Phones warm up when they charge, stream or run games, and some warmth is normal. Cooldown puts every battery reading on one scale, and alerts you when it reaches your alert threshold, 42°C by default.

Check it at a glance, or look back later.

Insights are part of Pro, or watch an optional ad to unlock them for 24 hours. Screens show example data.

No fake cooling.

  • It doesn't claim to cool your phone instantly.
  • It doesn't force-stop apps or lock CPU and GPU speeds.
  • It doesn't need root or read private system files.

Cooldown shows what is really heating your phone, and how to cool it down safely. Readings come from Android itself, so the available sensors vary by device.

Monitoring and alerts are free.

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes ads and adds temperature insights, detailed heat-risk signals, a custom alert threshold, color themes and transparent widgets.
