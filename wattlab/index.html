Three questions, answered.

The speed test is free and unlimited. Screens show readings recorded on one phone.

Battery health, with its error range.

Leave a charge plugged in until it finishes, such as overnight, and Wattlab reads your battery's full capacity. It shows how far the charges disagree and how many it rests on, and a health percentage only when the design capacity is known.

It keeps working after the test.

  • Every charge, recorded

    Turn on auto-record and each charge is logged in the background, from plug-in to unplug.

  • Battery usage

    How much battery you have used since unplugging, with screen-on and screen-off time shown apart, and a standby drain check.

  • Charge alerts

    A notification at 80%, or at any level you pick from 50% to 100%.

  • Home screen widget

    Charging watts and battery level, without opening the app.

It only measures.

  • It can't make charging faster or repair a battery, and it doesn't claim to.
  • No cleaner, no charging animations, no lock-screen overlays.
  • No made-up numbers. A value your phone doesn't report is shown as not reported.
  • No account and no cloud sync. Readings and charge history stay on your phone.

Phones report battery current in different units and signs. Wattlab checks how yours reports it before it shows a reading. Usage statistics send rounded ranges, never raw readings.
